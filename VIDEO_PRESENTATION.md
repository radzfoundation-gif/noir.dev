# 🎥 NOIR AI - Video Presentation Script

This document is designed as a storyboard and script for creating a high-quality demonstration video of the NOIR AI platform.

---

## 🎬 Scene 1: Introduction (The Vision)
**Visuals**: Slow zoom into the NOIR AI Logo with subtle background animations. 
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Welcome to NOIR AI. The future of web development starts here. We combine premium aesthetics with cutting-edge AI power to transform how you build interfaces."

---

## 🎨 Scene 2: The Interface (The Feel)
**Visuals**: Scroll through the dashboard, highlighting the glassmorphism effects, gradient backgrounds, and smooth hover animations on buttons.
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Designed with an elegant 'Noir' concept, our interface isn't just functional—it delivers a premium experience. Every element is built for both speed and beauty."

---

## 🪄 Scene 3: Design to Code (The Magic)
**Visuals**: 
1. Drag a website screenshot into the `ImageUpload` zone.
2. Select a model (e.g., Claude 3.5 Sonnet).
3. Click "Generate Code".
4. Show the code streaming in real-time.
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Witness the magic. Simply upload your design screenshot, select your AI brain of choice—from Claude to Gemini—and watch your design transform into clean, production-ready HTML and Tailwind CSS in seconds."

---

## 🧠 Scene 4: Multi-Model Intelligence (The Brains)
**Visuals**: Open the `ModelSelector` dropdown. Click through Claude Opus, Sonnet, and Gemini Flash.
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Flexibility is key. Whether you need high speed with Gemini 1.5 Flash, or maximum accuracy with Claude 3 Opus, NOIR AI puts you in full control of the intelligence you use."

---

## 💻 Scene 5: Live Preview & Code Editor
**Visuals**: Toggle between the "Code" view and the "Preview" tab. Hover over the "Copy Code" button.
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Don't just trust the code. See the results instantly in our Preview tab. Once satisfied, a single click copies production-grade code to your project."

---

## 🛠️ Scene 6: Technical Foundation
**Visuals**: Quick clips of the `.env` file, `package.json`, and the clean React component structure.
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Built on a modern stack—React 19, Vite, and Express—NOIR AI is engineered for high performance and future scalability."

---

## 🚀 Scene 7: Outro (Call to Action)
**Visuals**: Show the "Upgrade Pro" button with the Sparkles icon. Fade out to the NOIR AI logo.
**Audio/Script**: 
> "Elevate your productivity. Transforming design to code is now easier, faster, and more beautiful. This is NOIR AI. Start your journey today."

---

## 💡 Video Production Tips:
- **Background Music**: Use a deep, ambient synth or lo-fi track to match the "Noir" aesthetic.
- **Transitions**: Use smooth cross-fades or blur transitions.
- **Color Grading**: Keep it high contrast with vibrant accents (Violet/Lime) to highlight the UI.
